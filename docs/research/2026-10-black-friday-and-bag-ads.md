## 1. What BFCM Meta ads look like (fashion / accessories)

**Videos**
- Meta festive guide 2025: 9:16 with sound, mix at least 3 asset types; 9:16 + audio in Advantage+ sales = 7% more conversions per dollar (Meta's own data). [V] https://ppc.land/meta-releases-2025-festive-season-guide-with-ai-campaign-tools/
- Reels-first UGC demos and talking-to-camera; hook in 2 s; offer clear within 1-3 s; urgency in the creator's words, not countdown graphics; a bare "50% off" hook gets scrolled past. Agency opinion. [S] https://growthcart.beehiiv.com/p/black-friday-ads-what-s-working-right-now-on-meta-tiktok-and-google, https://influee.co/blog/black-friday-ad-examples
- Structure: hook 0-3 s (text in first frame, works muted), context 3-7 s, demo 8-20 s, proof 20-25 s, CTA text on screen 3 s+. Meta hook rate 25-30% baseline, 40%+ elite. Vendor figures. [S] https://segwise.ai/blog/anatomy-top-performing-video-ad-frame-by-frame.md
- Length: no paid evidence for 55-80 s. Meta says under 15 s tends to perform best in feed and gives no guidance above that; our format rests on the owner's own impression data. [V] https://inbeat.agency/blog/ugc-video-length-benchmark
- Reels-ad safe zone: keep text out of top 14%, bottom 35%, sides 6%. [S] https://adnova.ai/blogs/meta-ad-safe-zones-guide

## 3. Hook formulas for bag videos (you-voice, no invented experience)

Source lists give first-person lines ("I tried..."); rewrite them as claims about the bag. https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/ugc-ad-hook-examples, https://ecommercefastlane.com/ugc-hooks-for-ecommerce/ rate don't-buy-yet, contrarian, visual interrupt, big number, ranked list and POV as best for bags. No handbag-specific test data found [U].
- Visual interrupt (camera inside the bag as the zip opens): "Look at where this pocket sits."
- Problem-first: "Most shoulder bags open at the top, right behind you."
- Demonstration-first: "Watch what happens when the side zips close."
- Numbered: "Four things to look for in a bag you carry through crowds."
- POV: "POV: your hands stay free and your bag stays shut."
- Don't-buy-yet / contrarian: "Do not buy another crossbody before you see the back pocket."
- Travel: "Packing for a trip? Start with the bag."
- Gifting: "A gift that arrives with a free pouch wallet." (offer must match the live offer)
- Objection reply: "Are crossbody bags pickpocket-proof? Not all of them. Here's the difference." (a creator TikTok argues crossbodies "are not it": https://www.tiktok.com/@jeanlytravel/video/7224095320358030638?lang=en)
- Avoid absolutes such as "theft-proof"; use "designed to" and PLAYBOOK approved claims. [I]

## 4. Meta policy points

- **AI info label.** Applies to ad images created or materially edited with Meta's own generative AI tools; resize/colour correction doesn't count. Label sits in the three-dot menu, or next to "Sponsored" for a photorealistic AI human. [V] https://www.meta.com/en-gb/help/artificial-intelligence/355108217670024/
- **Third-party AI.** Meta's post, updated 1 June 2026, says it will begin auto-detecting ads made or edited with third-party AI tools "through industry-standard signals" and show "AI info" in a new "About this ad" menu. It names no signals, no advertiser disclosure duty, no penalty; varies by region. [V] https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/gen-ai-transparency-metas-ads-products/
- Our stills and clips come from non-Meta models, so assume they may be labelled. We show no photoreal humans, so the "Sponsored" placement shouldn't apply. [I] Blog claims that undisclosed AI causes 14% of rejections are untraced [U]: https://www.cinerads.com/blog/ai-ugc-facebook-ad-policy
- AI product images must show the real product (shape, colour, price). [S] https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/black-friday-ads-facebook
- **Sale and price claims.** Meta bans offers promoted through deceptive or misleading practices; the standards page doesn't mention strikethrough or countdowns. [V] https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/
- Strikethrough: no Meta rule found [U]. FTC benchmark: a former price must be the real price offered regularly for a reasonably substantial period; an inflated "was" price is deceptive. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2010-title16-vol1/pdf/CFR-2010-title16-vol1-sec233-1.pdf
- Fake urgency (resetting timers, never-changing "only 3 left") is named as a rejection reason by blogs [S] https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-ad-misleading-claims-personal-attributes-prohibited-content-policy-2026 and as deceptive by regulators https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2024/11/the-urgency-is-fake-but-the-deception-is-real.html
- Compliant urgency: real dates only ("Starts Thursday", "Ends Monday 11:59 PM") matching the landing page; offer, gift and shipping terms match the page. [I]
- **Reviews.** FTC rule (effective 21 Oct 2024) bans fake or AI-generated reviews; use only verbatim real ones. https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials
- **Personal attributes.** Ads can't assert or imply a person's attributes; "you/your" is fine when no attribute is attached. [V] https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/ , https://www.jonloomer.com/can-we-use-the-words-you-and-your-in-facebook-ads-copy/. For us: avoid "if you've been robbed", age, health, finances, "people like you". [I]
- **Text in image.** The 20% rule was dropped in 2020; Meta still recommends minimal text and heavy text can cut reach. [S] https://adzooma.com/blog/facebook-advertising-removes-20-text-rule-on-images
- Avoid big creative/targeting edits during BFCM peaks (learning-phase resets); build and schedule early. [S] https://ppc.land/meta-releases-2025-festive-season-guide-with-ai-campaign-tools/
