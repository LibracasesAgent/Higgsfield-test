## What they share

1. **Two kinds of winner, nothing in between.** Story ads of 60-105 s (198, 86(2), 33-H5, C105) and closers of 16-27 s (190-H3,
   113-H2: $27k between them, the 1st and the 8th ad by spend) [V]. No 30-50 s feature list is on the list. The closers explain
   nothing: 190-H3 says "this is Susan's closing sale" without saying who Susan is, so it is built for people who already saw a
   Susan story [I]. Story ads find the buyer; closers make her buy [I].
2. **The closer recipe (190-H3, 27 s):** a warning line ("Once this is gone, I promise you it's gone") → why now ("today is the
   absolute last day") → "Here's the secret": how to claim it (link → cart → checkout, "the 50% off applied automatically, plus a free
   matching wallet added to your order") → why it ends → "So don't wait." Every picture is the real bag (on a bench, being packed on
   a table, worn, the wallet going in) with 2-4 words of caption [V].
3. **Warning hooks win.** 190's five openings are all warnings ("Don't say I didn't warn you", "This is your final warning", "Once
   this is gone ..." (won), "This is your sign to finally stop waiting", "Whatever you do, don't miss this"); 198's five are all "before
   you buy" ("Do not buy my luxury hobo bag" (won), "Before you buy my bag, there's something I need you to know", "Hold on, before you
   buy this, hear me out", "This isn't the kind of bag you buy on impulse", "Before you click buy, let me be completely honest" (won))
   [V]. Add 158-H2 ("Before you buy one of my bags, I need to warn you"), C105 ("Don't do what I did") and 113-H2 ("This crazy deal ends
   tonight"). In 198-H1 the spoken line IS the on-screen headline ("Do NOT buy my Luxury Hobo Bag ❌", a red X over the bag held up to
   the camera) [V]. Our hook types (promise / question / problem / price) have no warning type.
4. **The winning presenter never cuts away.** In 198 and 33-H5 Susan is on screen the whole video: full-frame for her big lines,
   otherwise a cut-out (head to waist) in the lower half while the B-roll behind her changes every 1.7-2.3 s [V]. The pace stays
   fast and nothing jumps: what Julian asked for after B039 ("too many cuts and zoom-ins"). Our presenter cuts between her and
   full-screen B-roll.
5. **Many owners, shown as a grid.** 33-H5 and 198 put 2-4 different women wearing the bag in one frame on "why women have been
   choosing" / "women have carried mine" [V]. It shows many owners without one invented customer [I]; the library has the footage
   (worn 157+ moments).
6. **Type:** the hook headline at the top for the first 2-3 s with emoji and red (‼ THIS DEAL WON'T LAST ‼, ⚠ 50% OFF + FREE Wallet ⚠,
   Do NOT buy ❌); captions small, black on a white rounded box, mid-frame, 2-4 words [V]. 113-H2 keeps its banner all 16 s [V].
7. **Pace matches ours:** 174-224 wpm, a new shot every 1.7-2.7 s, -12.6 to -16 LUFS [V].
