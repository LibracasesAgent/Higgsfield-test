# Libra Cases: the last weeks of Q4 and which statics convert (research C, 2026-11-03)

Tags: [V] read on the page itself; [S] vendor or agency claim with no method shown; [I] my inference for Libra; [U] could not verify. The Meta Ad Library is not reachable from here. This file adds to `2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md` and doesn't repeat it. Libra facts: sale 23 Oct to 30 Nov; 50% off everything, up to 60% off bundles (only named bundles like the 3-Piece Set); site terms say "order by 14 December 2026 for the best chance of delivery before Christmas", not guaranteed; sale items are final sale (`pipeline/data/campaign.json`).

## 1. Q4 calendar: costs by week, what to say when, and what changes after 30 Nov

**Meta costs by week in 2025 (no source publishes one series for everyone; the baselines differ)**
- Biddy (US accounts that spent at least $30k in Oct, about $6.9M a month combined; each account against its own October; median of consumer brands). CPM / cost per purchase by week: 17-23 Nov +9% / -16%; 24-30 Nov (BFCM) +35% / -20%; 1-7 Dec (which held Cyber Monday, 1 Dec) +27% / -8%; **8-14 Dec +6% / +39%, ROAS -27%, the worst week of the season**; 15-21 Dec +3% / +8%; 22-28 Dec -8% / -26%; 29 Dec-4 Jan -24% / -24%. https://www.biddyco.com/blog-posts/meta-ads-black-friday-cpm [V]
- Lebesgue (largest spenders, $22.6M Sep-Nov 2025; median daily CPM against September): 10 Nov +13%, 20 Nov +27%, Thanksgiving +53%, Black Friday +84%, Cyber Monday +71%, the Tuesday after +48%, Wed 3 Dec +20%. Over Black Friday week, conversion rate rose 48%, cost per purchase fell 20% and ROAS rose 19%. https://lebesgue.io/facebook-ads/are-meta-ads-worth-it-black-friday [V]
- Pennock (peak week 24 Nov-1 Dec 2025 against October): Meta CPM went from $14.59 to $25.68 (+76%) and Meta ROAS from 3.19 to 4.68, while discounts went from 25.6% to 57.5% of sales. https://www.pennock.co/blog/2025-vs-2026-planning-for-holiday-season-for-dtc-brands [V]
- GeistM: CPM +11% for 16-30 Nov, +11% for 1-15 Dec and -10% for 16-31 Dec (-32% in 2024). Fashion & Beauty 1-15 Dec: CPM -17%, CPC -21%. No baseline or sample is given. https://geistm.com/blog/meta-rate-trends-q4-2025/ [S]
- What this means: the expensive auction of Black Friday week pays for itself through conversion rate. In the second week of December buyers are done and cost per purchase jumps, and late December is cheap. **For Libra, that worst week moves to 7-13 Dec in 2026, which is also its last week to sell for Christmas delivery (order by 14 Dec).** [I]

**Demand**
- Adobe (US, 1 Nov-31 Dec 2025): $257.8B in total. Cyber Week was $44.2B, about 17% of the season. Cyber Monday was the biggest day at $14.25B and Black Friday took $11.8B. 25 days passed $4B (18 in 2024). Mobile was 56.4% of sales, apparel discounts peaked at 25.1%, and curbside pickup peaked on 23 Dec at 39% of orders, as last-minute buyers ran out of shipping time. https://news.adobe.com/news/downloads/pdfs/2026/01/010726-2025-holiday-shopping-season.pdf [V]
- NRF survey of 1-10 Dec 2025 (n=8,005): shoppers were 51% done on average, and clothing and accessories were the top gift already bought (48%). In the October survey, 60% expected to finish in December. https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/a-record-159-million-consumers-expected-to-shop-on-super-saturday , https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/consumers-to-spend-second-highest-amount-on-record-according-to-nrf-holiday-survey [V]
- Meta's festive guide (16 Nov 2025, YouGov, self-reported): 15% of shoppers start in late November and 18% in early December. 50% discovered brands on Meta. https://ppc.land/meta-releases-2025-festive-season-guide-with-ai-campaign-tools/ [V]

**2026 dates.** Black Friday Fri 27 Nov; Cyber Monday Mon 30 Nov (the last sale day); Giving Tuesday 1 Dec; Green Monday Mon 14 Dec, the same day as Libra's order-by date. USPS lower-48 send-by dates: Ground Advantage and First-Class Thu 17 Dec, Priority Fri 18 Dec, Express Sat 19 Dec. Super Saturday 19 Dec; Christmas Fri 25 Dec. The later USPS dates do not extend Libra's own 14 Dec date. https://www.usps.com/holiday/holiday-shipping-dates.htm [V]

**What to say when**
- Meta's holiday guide (12 Aug 2026, Ipsos, n=14,473) splits the season into four phases. Discovery (Oct to early Nov). Deals (BFCM): carousel price comparisons, Stories with countdown timers, bold CTAs. Gifting (December): emotional video, carousel gift guides, Stories with real customer reactions. Fresh start (late Dec to New Year): aspirational Reels and retargeting of holiday browsers. Its timeline puts "last chance" messaging in December and asks for at least 3 formats. https://www.facebook.com/business/news/holiday-advantage-is-creative-advantage [V]
- Meta 2025: keep campaigns running into late December and January, when competitors pull back and CPMs are lower (no figures given). https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-holiday-marketing-ad-tips-2025-ai-ads/759347/ [V]
- Biddy: for 8-14 Dec, switch to gift and shipping-deadline messaging or cut budgets rather than run BFCM ads at BFCM spend. Late December suits prospecting and new tests. https://www.biddyco.com/blog-posts/meta-ads-black-friday-cpm [S]
- Common Thread: before the shipping cutoff, push "order ahead of the deadline"; after it, push gift cards. Q5 (after Christmas) is the third-largest new-customer spike of H2 and has some of the cheapest CPMs. https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/holiday-marketing-ecommerce-strategy-2024 [S]
- Chronos's 2026 calendar: 1-2 Dec "extended 24 hours" on the same offer; 14 Dec Green Monday; 15-19 Dec "It's not too late" with a "Ships by Dec 17" banner; gift cards from 26 Dec. It claims 22% extra revenue after BFCM with no method. https://chronos.agency/blog/weve-run-bfcm-offers-for-500-dtc-brands-heres-what-works-before-and-after-black-friday/ [S]

**What brands did**
- BFCM 2025, Common Thread (147 clients, $127M revenue, 5.1 MER): several mini-peaks (a VIP Thursday, a Saturday flash sale, a bigger Monday), one offer messaged in several ways rather than a new offer each time, and a pre-built "parachute" offer ready to swap in. https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/dtc-hotline/47-brands-127m-what-worked-bfcm-2025 [S]
- BFCM 2025 brand survey: statics led. Proven winners with the sale message added were among the best ads, and promo creative "outside our usual brand world" failed. https://new-edition-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/new-edition-s-bfcm-2025-brand-insights-report [S]
- December 2024: Geologie and Foria set their own cutoffs (16-18 Dec), then sent late shoppers to Amazon and retail partners. Manscaped ran a "diminishing discount" that started at 25% and shrank toward Christmas. https://www.modernretail.co/operations/dtc-brands-are-promoting-their-amazon-stores-to-support-faster-holiday-delivery/ [V]
- No public teardown of bag or accessory brands' December 2025 Meta ads was found (searched Motion's brand library, Modern Retail, DTC Newsletter and agency recaps; the Ad Library is unreachable). https://motionapp.com/library/dagne-dover [U]

**When the sale ends on 30 Nov**
- Give every sale ad and sale end card ("Black Friday", "Cyber Monday", "up to 60%") an end time of 30 Nov 23:59 in the ad account's time zone. In catalog overlays, the sale-price label drops by itself when the catalog's sale window ends, and Ads Manager does not warn you. https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-catalog-ad-overlays [S]
- On 1 Dec, relaunch the proven winners with the regular offer and a gift line: keep the body, change the copy and the CTA. https://new-edition-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/new-edition-s-bfcm-2025-brand-insights-report [S]
- If the price after 30 Nov stays at the sale price, don't say "the sale has ended", and don't show a struck-through "was" price unless it is a real earlier price. The FTC requires a former price to be the actual, bona fide price, offered regularly for a reasonably substantial period. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2010-title16-vol1/pdf/CFR-2010-title16-vol1-sec233-1.pdf [V]

## 2. Static formats: what the evidence says (2025-2026)

- Motion Creative Benchmarks 2026 (578,750 creatives, 6,015 accounts, $1.29B, 1 Sep 2025-1 Jan 2026, so BFCM is in the data). "Hit rate" is the share of a format's ads that reach at least 10x the account's median spend: it measures where Meta puts spend, not ROAS. **Offer-first banner**: 8.6% hit rate, 21.9% of creatives but 29.3% of spend (1.3x). Testimonial 6.5% (1.0x), Demo 8.1%, Unboxing 9.8% (only 2.1% of volume). https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-visual-formats [V]
- Asset types ranked by hit rate: 1 text only, 2 product image with text, 3 lifestyle-product image, 4 UGC, 5 high production, 13 product image without text, 15 (last) carousel. Carousel is also last on spend use. Hit rates run about 4-12%. https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-asset-types [V]
- Fashion & Apparel's top formats by hit rate: Post-it, Quiz, Stylized product shot, Meme, ASM, Product shot, Social comment, Podcast, Product showcase, Unconventional text placement. By spend use: Podcast, Unconventional text placement, Billboard, Text message, Sign, Celebrity, Slideshow, Post-it, Offer-first banner, Demo. Founder ads are not in fashion's list (they lead Health & Wellness). Accessories has no vertical of its own. https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/visual-formats-by-vertical [V]
- For Libra: the bold HALF PRICE poster is the data's strongest static for the sale. A packshot with no words ranks near the bottom, so give every real-photo static one short line. Formats to invent: a post-it note on the bag, a billboard or street sign (no real brand), unusual text placement, a quiz ("Which strap is hers?"). A "social comment" ad may only use a real review, word for word, set as type; never a fake app screen. [I]
- Practitioner lists of proven DTC statics (Us vs Them, Problem-Solution, Demo, Testimonials, Reasons Why) give no comparative figures. One framework rates testimonial statics as cheap to make and slow to fatigue. https://segwise.ai/blog/100m-meta-ads-5-formats-dtc , https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-ad-creative-testing-framework [S]
- Lo-fi: Pilothouse says a phone-shot photo with holiday décor sometimes beats polished studio images. Brainlabs (46 Meta brand-lift studies, 225 UK campaigns): lo-fi native creative moved purchase intent most, and polished creative moved awareness. https://www.directtoconsumer.co/newsletter/dtc940-gifting-angles-gifting-content-framework-and-non-discount-bfcm-offer-ideas , https://www.brainlabsdigital.com/meta-brand-lift-studies-objectives-creatives-results/ [S]
- AI vs real photos, "AI in Disguise" (Columbia and Taboola; 4,633 matched ads from 202 brands, 369M impressions): AI images did as well as human ones overall. CTR by group: AI that looks human-made 0.79%, human-made that doesn't look AI 0.67%, AI that looks AI 0.62%, human-made that looks AI 0.55%. Looking artificial costs the clicks, not being AI. https://mediacat.uk/disguised-ai-ads-outperform-human-ones/amp/ , https://www.columbia.edu/~on2110/Papers/AI_in_disguise.pdf [V]
- A vendor benchmark (50,000 variations) claims AI creative gets 12% higher CTR on Meta but converts 8% worse above a $100 order value. Libra's ~$84 order sits just below that. https://www.adbeacon.com/ai-creative-wins-clicks-loses-conversions/ [S]
- AI label: since its 1 Jun 2026 update, Meta auto-detects ads made with third-party AI tools through industry signals (C2PA) and shows "AI info" under "About this ad". It has published no data on performance effects. https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/gen-ai-transparency-metas-ads-products/ , https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/meta-ai-ad-labels-mandatory-disclosure-ecommerce-2026 [V]
- Aspect ratios: Meta recommends 4:5 for Facebook and Instagram Feed, Explore and Marketplace. A Meta Client Solutions Manager reported 5-13% higher CTR than 1:1 or 1.91:1 in tests. Carousel cards stay 1:1. https://thekeyword.co/news/meta-enables-advertisers-to-use-a-4-5-aspect-ratio-for-ads [S]
- Placements: Meta's festive guide asks for 9:16 video with sound and at least 3 asset types (statics, carousels, Reels). https://ppc.land/meta-releases-2025-festive-season-guide-with-ai-campaign-tools/ [V]
- Text density: Meta sets no text limit. Under Andromeda, variants that differ only in their overlay text count as one creative, so new ads need a new picture or concept, not new words. https://www.superads.ai/blog/creative-diversity-in-ads [S]
- Volume: top-quartile accounts launch 2-3x more creatives than peers in their spend tier, and about half of all ads get little or no spend. https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/ad-creative-volume-benchmarks [S]

## 3. Gift creative in December

- NRF, October 2025 (n=8,247): the most-wanted gifts are gift cards (50%) and clothing or accessories (46%). https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/consumers-to-spend-second-highest-amount-on-record-according-to-nrf-holiday-survey [V]
- Meta: 48% want gift ideas from brands on Reels, and 55% prefer Reels for festive recommendations. A bag gift idea should also run as a 9:16 Reel. https://ppc.land/meta-releases-2025-festive-season-guide-with-ai-campaign-tools/ [V]
- Meta's 2026 guide: turn existing product photos into a carousel gift guide, run emotional video, and show real customer reactions. https://www.facebook.com/business/news/holiday-advantage-is-creative-advantage [V]
- Pilothouse (10 Nov 2025): launch gift creative during Black Friday, not after it, so December has proven ads. Put gift headlines ("The Perfect Gift for Every Occasion", "Make Their Day with a [product]") on the best formats; use a self-gift angle when the product isn't an obvious gift. The podcast says gift guides "don't work well". https://www.directtoconsumer.co/newsletter/dtc940-gifting-angles-gifting-content-framework-and-non-discount-bfcm-offer-ideas [S]
- The sources disagree on gift guides: Meta pushes carousel gift guides, while Pilothouse and Motion's carousel ranking do not. Test one carousel gift guide against single-image gift statics. [I]
- The claim that "gift-context creative beats product ads by 30-50%" is repeated by several blogs; I found no source for it. https://topgrowthmarketing.com/christmas-meta-ads-examples/ [U]
- Unboxing has the highest hit rate in Motion's list (9.8%). Show a real gift-unboxing or packaging photo only if Libra really ships that packaging: an AI picture of packaging Libra doesn't use would be a false claim. https://runneth.com/library/formats/ [V]
- Gift cards are the agencies' standard move after the cutoff, but libracases.com/products/gift-card returns 404, so Libra probably sells none. If so, after 14 Dec the gift ads become "a gift for the new year" or a self-gift. https://libracases.com/products/gift-card [U]
- Religion: Libra's rules ban religious content. Use "Christmas delivery" only as a date, with no religious symbols; Meta's calendar lists Hanukkah, so say "the holidays" in general lines. https://ppc.land/meta-releases-2025-festive-season-guide-with-ai-campaign-tools/ [I]

## 4. Urgency that is true and compliant

- Meta: "The products and services promoted in an ad must match those promoted on the landing page", and its review may include the landing page. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/ [V]
- Meta: ads must not promote offers "using identified deceptive or misleading practices". https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/ad-standards/fraud-scams/unacceptable-business-practices [V]
- Meta itself recommends Stories with countdown timers in the deals phase. Use one only when it counts down to the real end (30 Nov 23:59) and the site shows the same date. https://www.facebook.com/business/news/holiday-advantage-is-creative-advantage [V]
- The FTC's dark-patterns report (Sep 2022) calls baseless countdown timers and false low-stock or high-demand messages deceptive. True ones are fine. https://www.venable.com/insights/blogs/2022/09/the-ftc-brings-more-light-to-dark-patterns-in-new [S]
- Ads & Scale: there is no reliable lift figure for showing a cutoff date. "Order by Thursday for Christmas delivery" is a fact, not manufactured scarcity, and holds up better with returning customers. https://www.adsandscale.com/blog/holiday-shipping-cutoffs-delivery-promise [S]
- Libra's true urgencies: the sale ends 30 Nov (the repo blocks "last chance / final days" until 20 Nov, and "ends tonight / today only" except on 30 Nov). "Order by 14 December for the best chance of delivery before Christmas" is usable; "guaranteed" is not. Libra has no stock data, so never "only N left" or "selling fast". `docs/PLAYBOOK.md` §4f [V]
- Returns: sale orders are final sale until 30 Nov (`pipeline/data/campaign.json`, [V]). A "gift returns" line in December only if Libra's regular policy really offers it: I did not check the site. https://libracases.com [U]

## 5. Carousel, collection and catalog ads for bundles and colours

- Motion ranks carousel last of 15 asset types for both hit rate and spend use: Meta rarely gives carousels the spend. https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-asset-types [V]
- "Carousels get 30-50% lower CPA in retargeting, per Meta": repeated on vendor pages, with no Meta source found. https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/carousel-ads-strategy-ecommerce [U]
- A vendor benchmark puts single image at 1.74% CTR and 3.3 ROAS against carousel at 2.08% and 3.7, with no method. https://mhigrowthengine.com/blog/single-image-ads-vs-carousel-ads-ecommerce/ [S]
- Dynamic overlays (24 Apr 2025): Advantage+ catalog images can carry current price, struck-through price, % off and free shipping, each switched on or off; Facebook Feed only; no performance data. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-advantage-plus-catalog-ads-overlay-offers/746337/ [V]
- Catalog results vary widely: in Vaizle's 30 tests across 9 accounts (15 Oct-11 Dec 2025), a footwear catalog made 5.72 ROAS while two other catalogs made 0 purchases. https://insights.vaizle.com/post-andromeda-performance-report/ [V]
- Collection ads: only vendor claims, no independent data. https://www.attnagency.com/blog/meta-collection-ads-ecommerce [U]
- Libra use: one carousel only where swiping is the point. The 3-Piece Set: card 1 is the set with its % and works on its own, then one 1:1 real packshot per colour. Run it mainly to warm audiences. Single images and Reels do the prospecting. [I]

## Top 10 takeaways for Libra

1. The last profitable stretch is 23 Nov-7 Dec. The second week of December was the worst of 2025 (cost per purchase +39%), and in 2026 it is also Libra's last gift-shipping week. Spend there only on warm audiences and a true deadline. https://www.biddyco.com/blog-posts/meta-ads-black-friday-cpm [V]
2. Libra's deadline is 14 Dec, earlier than the USPS dates (17-19 Dec). From 15 Dec on, no ad may suggest Christmas delivery. https://www.usps.com/holiday/holiday-shipping-dates.htm [V]
3. The offer-first banner is the static Meta funds most (29.3% of spend from 21.9% of creatives). Keep the HALF PRICE poster through 30 Nov, then swap the words for the regular offer and the gift line. https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-visual-formats [V]
4. Product images with text beat product images without (rank 2 against 13): no static should be a wordless packshot. https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-asset-types [V]
5. Fashion's top formats are Post-it, Quiz, Stylized product shot, Social comment and Unconventional text placement. Make them Libra's new AI ad formats; a social comment only from a real, verbatim review. https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/visual-formats-by-vertical [V]
6. AI statics are fine as long as they don't look AI: AI that reads as real out-clicked real photos (0.79% against 0.67%), and anything that looks artificial loses. Keep the realism gate strict. https://mediacat.uk/disguised-ai-ads-outperform-human-ones/amp/ [V]
7. Re-run the 2.9-ROAS winners: with the sale line until 30 Nov, then the gift line from 1 Dec. Proven bodies with a new message were the 2025 pattern. https://new-edition-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/new-edition-s-bfcm-2025-brand-insights-report [S]
8. Launch gift creative now, during the sale, so December starts with ads that have data. https://www.directtoconsumer.co/newsletter/dtc940-gifting-angles-gifting-content-framework-and-non-discount-bfcm-offer-ideas [S]
9. Carousels rank last in Motion's data: one 3-Piece Set / colours carousel and one gift-guide test, both 1:1, mainly for warm audiences. Statics should be 4:5, plus 9:16 Reels. https://thekeyword.co/news/meta-enables-advertisers-to-use-a-4-5-aspect-ratio-for-ads [S]
10. Every urgency line must be true and match the site: the sale end on 30 Nov 23:59, "order by 14 December for the best chance". No stock claims, no "guaranteed", no "was" price that wasn't real; and plan the late-December fresh-start push, when CPMs fall 8-24%. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/ [V]
