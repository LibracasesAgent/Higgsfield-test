# Christmas statics and b-roll for bags (research 2026-11-24)

Asked by the owner after B054-B056: "statics are very basic, simple things ... we need Christmas themed ones ... it should not be too much";
"the B-roll should match the voiceover" (a line about a tote must show a tote, not our bag).

## What Christmas ads for bags look like
- [V] Coach Holiday 2025 "The Gift for New Adventures": vignettes, each scene one holiday moment, the bag as the winter companion and
  the gift (theimpression.com, luxferity.com, jingdaily.com).
- [V] Kate Spade Holiday 2025 "Spark Something Beautiful": snowy streets, twinkling lights, playful details (finanzen.net release).
- [S] DTC holiday statics that work: the product shown AS a gift (wrapped, in hands, in a box) with explicit "for her" copy; a bold
  offer overlay on a product hero; high contrast and a legible offer beat muted aesthetic layouts in the urgent weeks
  (topgrowthmarketing.com Christmas Meta ads; newengen.com Holiday Creative Playbook 2025).
- [S] Gift-guide ads run mid-November to early December, median 23 days (admakeai.com, 573 holiday ads from the Meta Ad Library).
- [S] Value-add (a free gift with the bag) reads stronger than a plain discount (topgrowthmarketing.com).
- [I] "Not too much": one or two Christmas cues per picture (a ribbon, a pine sprig, warm string-light bokeh, kraft paper, snow on a
  window), never a full Santa / tinsel scene; the bag stays the hero and closed.

## Rules taken from it
1. Every static is a Christmas MOMENT with the real bag in it (under the tree, in tissue paper, on a gift tag, a snowy street from
   behind, a wishlist, the morning she opens it), the offer in the design, one short line. No templated colour / value cards.
2. Every b-roll shot shows what its words say. A scene line (station, market stall, office party, a tote) needs that scene;
   the library has none of them, so they are AI scene clips (the real bag in the scene, people from behind, no faces), a feature
   line (pocket, zip, strap) keeps real footage of exactly that.
