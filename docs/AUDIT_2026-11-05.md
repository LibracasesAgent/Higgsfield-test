# Pipeline audit, 2026-11-05: why ads went wrong, what was slow, what changed

Scope: batches B011-B038 (20 Oct - 4 Nov): docs/BATCHES.md, the ledger, every plan / compliance / presenter-gate file in
`pipeline/recipes/`, the git history (74 commits), 14 Drive run reports, and the code paths behind each failure.

## 1. Why the pipeline "hallucinated" (root causes)

| # | Root cause | Evidence | Fixed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Claims were checked by blocklist, never against the facts.** Any property word not on the compliance list passed: "lockable" for a bag without lockable zips, "blocks card scanners" on a bag without RFID, "fits an iPad", "holds everything". | B025 P01 said "lockable" for the 3-Piece Set; compliance.json shows 0 problems in all 21 plans, yet people caught invented copy later | `rulebook.json claims`: 12 claim families that pass only when the product's facts say them |
| 2 | **Nothing judged hook craft.** A hook only had to be ≤14 words with a self-declared `type`; no check for specificity, formula, generic openers or a type that fits its words. | B030-B034: 14 hooks opened "Black Friday prices are live now ..."; "The Hobo Bag page calls this set the Best Value."; "question" hooks with no question | `playbook_check.py` hook rules: concrete + formula (Halbert / winners), opener reuse over 8 batches, type vs words |
| 3 | **Counts licensed themselves.** "Five things ..." was allowed because the sheet itself contained "five". | `ideas.py` built `allowed` from the sheet's own count words | `count_delivered`: a hook's count must equal the numbered chapters |
| 4 | **Third-person / named stories passed.** Only first person was caught. | "Her keys are at the bottom of the bag again." (B032) | `invented_customer` (hard), `hook_third_person` (soft) |
| 5 | **Urgency in our own templates.** `ad_copy.py` and the Black Friday theme close said "before it's gone". | ad_copy.py:72, make_batch THEMES | scarcity rule + templates reworded |
| 6 | **Terms changed after ads were made** (gifts dropped, final sale, a price change). | 32 videos said "easy returns"; 6 statics printed an old price | `price_figure` (hard on hooks / statics / headlines), `free_returns` claim rule; campaign.json stays the source for sale terms |
| 7 | **The rules lived in prose.** PLAYBOOK.md is 900 lines of history; the strategist could not tell a current rule from a superseded one. | the 14 Oct "hook test" (29 s videos) against rule 6 | `docs/RULEBOOK.md` (ranked, current) + `rulebook.json` (enforced), printed in every strategy packet |

## 2. Why the footage did not match the captions (root causes)

| # | Root cause | Fixed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shots were chosen for estimated phrases and placed by group index.** `ugc_board` estimates word timings (2.75 w/s × 1.12); at render the real voice and a story beat's pace regroup the phrases, and `plan_block` filled the new groups with `body[gi]`: the "phone" pick landed under "the back pocket ...". | `storyboard.match_pick`: before each shot is cut, the words under it are checked against its picture; a pick that shows none of them swaps with a later pick or a spare that does |
| 2 | **`choose()` had no floor.** Reuse costs (7) outweighed match weights (2-5), so a wrong shot beat a right one shown before; it returned the best entry however negative. | strict mode: a phrase naming a feature only takes a labelled moment that shows it (falls back only when the library has none) |
| 3 | **Thin footage was a warning.** A line about the snap of the Hobo Bag (0 good snap moments) planned fine and got a stand-in. | `ideas.check`: refused when every feature the words name has < 4 good moments (`footage_problem`) |
| 4 | **Nothing checked picture vs words after render.** The scorecard measures pace and loudness only; mismatches went to Drive. | `make_batch.footage_match`: every shot vs its caption words on the exact frames; `shot mismatch` is HARD (not uploaded) |
| 5 | **False wants.** "a coffee in one hand" asked for the clasp; `handheld` / `outdoor` were missing from the vocabulary. | `shotmatch.wants` fixed, both tags added, `ALSO` near-equivalents (compartments ~ inner pocket) |

Measured on the committed specs of 25 Oct - 4 Nov (block level): 16 of 315 judged shots showed none of what their block said, e.g.
"One hand on the clasp, and it snaps shut" over a strap shot (B028), "the pouch keeps cards and cash" over the bag on a chair (B025).

Still open (listed, not blocked): shots from unlabelled sources (photos, AI clips, new products' clips, the 3-Piece Set, which has no
clips of its own) are `footage unverified`; label them (`footage_index.py label-sheets`) to bring them under the blocker. Library gaps
(good moments): Hobo Bag snap 0, tie 0, crowd 0, lock 1; Hobo 2.0 snap 0, street 0; Vintage side zip 0; Slouchy snap / lock 0.

## 3. Blockers and time sinks (ranked by frequency × cost)

| Blocker | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Video face check holding videos (mostly false hits on hands, key rings, back views) | B011-13: 21 of 36 held; B025 16, B035 12, B034 6 re-rendered (~20-25 min a project each) | threshold raised before (0.88, 2+ frames); **next:** run the face check per library clip at index time on more frames so a held shot is swapped at plan, not after render |
| Offer / terms changing after ads were made | B019-B027 remade; 32 videos with a false returns line | now caught at plan (rules 6 above); keep `campaign.json` the single source |
| Batch numbers / manual merges / two runs of one bag at once | 7 burned numbers, 3 manual merges | fixed 25 Oct (Drive lock, auto-merge) |
| Upload deleting the shared shot cache during a render | B016: 2 videos failed | fixed (upload keeps `_seg` while anything is pending) |
| Offer-card anchor missing ("half price" without the %) | 8 videos without the offer card | fixed 27 Oct (the offer chapter must say the %) |
| AI static gate failures (OCR misreads, tiny handwriting) | 2-4 per statics batch | open: soft |
| Re-renders after QA (repeats, body differs from H1) | ~1 project a batch | the footage blocker now names the bad shot (qa/<id>_footage.json), so a fix is one spec edit + `render --only` |

Time per batch now: statics a few minutes; ~20-25 min per 4-version project (2 at a time); a full 5-project batch ~2 h; a presenter
project adds Kling takes (~42 takes for B016). The biggest remaining saving is the face check (above): every held video costs a full
re-render of its project's versions.

## 4. What changed today

- `docs/RULEBOOK.md` + `pipeline/data/rulebook.json`: the system rulebook (targets incl. hook rate 40-45%, hook formulas from Halbert's
  349 headlines and our winners, banned phrases, claim words that need facts, footage rules, presenter count, what won / lost).
- `pipeline/playbook_check.py`: the checker, run by `ideas.py check` (hard rules stop the plan for sheets from 2026-11-05),
  `ad_copy.py` (failing template lines skipped) and `make_batch.py check` / plan / render (ad copy problems join compliance.json).
- The footage blocker in three gates (plan, render, after render; `shot mismatch` is HARD).
- The presenter in about half the projects (2 → 1, 3-4 → 2, 5 → 3), configurable.
- `pipeline/weekly_review.py`: Meta export in, a meeting report out (`docs/reviews/<week>.md`), one-line rulebook updates with a changelog.
- The strategy packet prints the rulebook and the last weeks' best / worst hooks.

## 5. Recommended next steps

1. **Feed real numbers every week.** No hook rate has ever been recorded in the repo; the tracker's ROAS columns were dropped because
   nobody typed in them. One Ads Manager export a week (`weekly_review.py ingest`) makes the rulebook learn.
2. Label the unlabelled sources (the 3-Piece Set has no clips; new products' clips) so the footage blocker covers them.
3. Shoot / source the missing moments: Hobo Bag snap and lock, crowds, a street walk for the Hobo 2.0, the Vintage side view.
4. Move the face check earlier (index time, 8 frames per clip) to stop the post-render re-render loop.
