## P01 · A packed Saturday market
**Big idea:** One real moment, start to finish: a packed Saturday market, your hands full, and the bag keeping your cards against you and your keys one pull away.
**Why it is new:** The first Hobo video that is one moment told start to finish, in a crowd, leading with the hidden back pocket as the anti-theft design it is, and the sale only at the end.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [question] `CROWDED?`: Heading somewhere crowded this weekend? Here's where your cards should go.  _(picture: pocket, zip_hand)_
- H2 [promise] `ANTI-THEFT`: It's an anti-theft bag. It just doesn't look like one.  _(picture: worn, walking)_
- H3 [problem] `HANDS FULL`: Hands full in a crowd, and your cards in an outside pocket?  _(picture: side_zip, zip_hand)_
- H4 [price] `50% OFF`: Fifty percent off, and your cards sit flat against you.  _(picture: strap, worn)_
