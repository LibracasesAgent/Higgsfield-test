## P01 · POV: out the door, coffee in hand
**Big idea:** POV: you are leaving the house with a coffee in one hand, and the bag still has to close, go on, and come with you down the street.
**Why it is new:** No Vintage video put the viewer inside the moment (POV); it opens on the coffee and the hand at the clasp, the moment the B028 test proved, told as a scene you step into.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [promise] `ONE HAND`: If you can hold a coffee, you can close this bag.  _(picture: snap, handheld)_
- H2 [question] `COFFEE DOWN?`: How often do you put your coffee down just to close your bag?  _(picture: handheld, snap)_
- H3 [problem] `HANDS FULL`: Coffee in one hand, keys in the other, bag still open.  _(picture: worn, walking)_
- H4 [price] `50% OFF`: Fifty percent off, and it closes with the one hand you have left.  _(picture: strap, worn)_

## P02 · One bag, four places
**Big idea:** One ordinary day in four short places (the car, the cafe, the errands, dinner), and in each one the bag does one small thing right.
**Why it is new:** The first Vintage video told across four places; it leads with what is inside (no digging), where the earlier stories led with the clasp or the strap.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [promise] `NO DIGGING`: Car, cafe, errands, dinner: watch what you never have to dig for.  _(picture: inside, packing)_
- H2 [question] `KEYS AGAIN?`: Do you dig for your keys every time you get out of the car?  _(picture: handheld, car)_
- H3 [promise] `FOUR PLACES`: Four places in one day, and one bag that keeps up.  _(picture: worn, street)_
- H4 [price] `HALF PRICE`: Half price, and your phone, wallet and keys each get their own spot.  _(picture: packing, inside)_
