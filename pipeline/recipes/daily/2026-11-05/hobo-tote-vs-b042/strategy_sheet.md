# Strategy sheet: Hobo Bag · black friday · 2026-11-05

## Research and what it changed

- Julian 2026-11-05 (call): 'us versus them is a proven concept ... you have to show them: a tote bag with a grey filter, a bit green, pixelated, low quality; the Hobo Bag in nice colours'. B037 S17 showed only our bag: every static here shows the tote too (story versus: brand_statics.them_half; split_compare: the format's new art).
- Oct 6 - Nov 4 export: our only AI video winner is B017 P04 'an ordinary bag vs the Hobo 2.0' (ROAS 2.79-2.97): contrast beats lists. Question hooks 52% hook rate vs offer hooks 43%: no sale in the hooks of this batch.
- 'Retire the old one' (B016 P04 H4) had a 52% hook rate: retire-the-tote is one of the static ideas.
- Our best statics are simple real-photo layouts (S09_pick_your_colour ROAS 2.93, S07_set3_value 2.57): the batch keeps a colours and a value static.
- Julian on statics: a catchy benefit headline (never a feature name), the offer once and big, no review card, the bag big with no empty space, text never over the strap.
- Footage (packet section 2): strap 350, zip 248, side_zip 200, pocket 197, worn 157, inside 111, walking 51; lock 1, snap 0, crowd 0: no line leans on a lock, the clasp or a crowd shot. The library has no tote footage, so the videos make the comparison in words (as B017 P04 did) over the Hobo Bag proving each point; the statics show the tote.
