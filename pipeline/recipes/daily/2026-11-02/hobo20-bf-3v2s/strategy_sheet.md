# Strategy sheet: Hobo 2.0 · black friday · 2026-11-02

## Research and what it changed

- Read the strategy packet (true facts, the Black Friday Sale 2026 campaign facts, footage tags, B013 / B015 / B017) and docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: demonstration-first and POV hooks beat a bare '50% off' open; the sale goes last; the hand-reach and one-moment concepts; Pacsafe-style anti-theft ads lean on a crowd scenario.
- Earlier Hobo 2.0 batches were a day, a demo, the chair strap, layers, four questions, a gift guide, a weekend pack and a versus; none used a one-moment story, advice to a friend led by the two straps, or a pass/fail test led by the hidden pockets, so those are the three structures; the lead features differ (zips, two straps, hidden pockets).
- Offer: the Hobo 2.0 keeps its own 50% and its own free pouch wallet (the offer card); 'up to sixty percent off' is said only as the campaign's sale line; no free gifts, no bundle claim, no returns wording (final sale), no countdown or 'ends tonight', no exact prices (price test).
- Footage coverage for the Hobo 2.0: strap 171, home 146, zip 122, side_zip 94, pocket 92, packing 81, worn 50, cafe 49, lock 24, tie 20, walking 6 (thin, so 'worn' carries the person shots); the bag on a person is worn / strap shots, never a face.
- Statics: one AI-designed ad in a format of my own (a cafe chalkboard sign) and one real native frame; the ad's words carry the % only, no price figures.

## P01 · The packed cafe
**Big idea:** One Saturday-morning moment in a packed cafe queue: the bag stays zipped on your shoulder, you never check it, and the sale only closes the story.
**Why it is new:** Earlier Hobo 2.0 batches walked a day (B013), looped the table strap (B015), answered four questions (B017) or compared with an ordinary bag; this is one moment told start to finish, led by the lockable zips, with the zips' own sound as the proof and no list.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [question] `CROWD CHECK`: How many times do you check your zip in a crowd?  _(picture: zip_hand, lock)_
- H2 [problem] `PACKED CAFE`: Squeezing through a packed cafe with your bag open behind you.  _(picture: worn, cafe)_
- H3 [promise] `ZIPS CLIP`: Watch these two zips clip together. Then try to get in.  _(picture: side_zip, handheld)_

## P03 · The keys test
**Big idea:** A live test any bag can try: find your phone, your cards and your keys one after another without looking inside, then zip it shut and sit down.
**Why it is new:** B013 and B017 showed the pockets as a list or a packing scene; this is a pass-or-fail test led by the hidden pockets, with the pocket pull as the proof and the table strap as the way it ends.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [question] `KEYS TEST`: Can you find your keys without looking in your bag?  _(picture: pocket, zip_hand)_
- H2 [problem] `STOP DIGGING`: Digging to the bottom of your bag for a pair of keys. Again.  _(picture: packing, inside)_
- H3 [promise] `WHERE IT GOES`: Phone, cards, keys. Watch where each one goes.  _(picture: side_zip, handheld)_
- H4 [price] `BLACK FRIDAY`: Black Friday prices are live now, and this bag passes the keys test.  _(picture: worn, strap)_
