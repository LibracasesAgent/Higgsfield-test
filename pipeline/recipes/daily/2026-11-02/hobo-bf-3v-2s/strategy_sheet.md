## Research and what it changed

- Strategy packet + docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: demonstrations and a packing-a-scenario opening (Dagne Dover, Bellroy) beat a bare '50% off' open; one big visual and next to no text for statics; real-looking candid photos (cafe table concept) beat glossy.

**Why it is new:** No earlier Hobo batch told the night before: this one is one evening and one morning, led by the divided compartments, with the zip closing as the held proof shot and the sale only as the last line.
