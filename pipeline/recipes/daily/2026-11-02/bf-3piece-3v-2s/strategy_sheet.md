## Research and what it changed

- Read the strategy packet: B025 (25 Oct) used the angles youandher, livenow, giftlist and inthebox and statics bundle / concept / value / studio / gifts / checklist / colours, so no gift list, no 'one for you, one for her', no 'live now' and no walk through the box this time.
- Read docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: bare '50% off' hooks get scrolled past, Dagne Dover / Bellroy win on packing and scenario demos, Pacsafe on 'what is different about your bag' comparisons, flat lays beat lifestyle for a handbag store; so the videos open on a moment and the sale is the last chapter.
- Checked the live product page (libracases.com, 2 Nov): three pieces, the Hobo Bag with smart compartments and anti-theft design, the Crossbody for hands-free ease, the Mini Pouch for quick essentials, soft water-resistant scratch-proof leather; this gave each project a different lead feature (crossbody, anti-theft design, pouch) and three story shapes none of the earlier batches used (four places, what you carry now, the night before).
- Statics: the product's last batch used no AI ad formats, so S01 takes the owner's favourite flat lay (the three pieces side by side, closed) and S02 is a format of our own, three shop tags that give each piece its job in the facts' own words (hobo for every day, crossbody to go light, pouch for quick essentials).

**Big idea:** A day in four stops, and each stop uses a different piece of the set: the hobo out the door, the crossbody on the move, the pouch at lunch.
**Why it is new:** Earlier 3-Piece Set videos were gifts, a sale announcement and a walk through the box; this one is a day told in four places, led by the crossbody, with the sale only in the last line.
