## Research and what it changed

- docs/research/2026-11-winners-30d.md: the account's biggest ad of the last 30 days, ACH-SVIT-190-H3 ($19.9k, ROAS 2.36), is a 27 s closer: a warning line, why now, 'here's the secret' (how to get the offer), the free wallet, why it ends, 'don't wait'; real footage of the bag, no features. 113-H2 (16 s, ROAS 2.55) is the same idea in one shot. - What they never copy: 190's 'today is the absolute last day' (said daily for weeks: the sale ends on 30 November, PLAYBOOK 2026-10-27) and 'Susan's closing sale' (invented): the deadline here is the real one, and no returns promise (Black Friday orders are final sale).
