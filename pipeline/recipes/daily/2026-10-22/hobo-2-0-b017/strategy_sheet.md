# Strategy sheet: Hobo 2.0 · evergreen · 2026-10-22

## Research and what it changed

- CLIENT_GUIDE request row: 2 presenter + 2 voiceover projects, each with its own story; the presenter is a host in 'you' form, never a customer (CLAUDE.md rule 1).
- Packet (B013 day/demo, B015 chairtie/layers): earlier Hobo 2.0 batches did a day, a demo, the chair strap and a layers build; none did an objection Q&A, a buyer's guide, a packing list or an ordinary-vs-this comparison, and none sat the host down.
- docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: Pacsafe-style pointouts and 'us vs them' (no competitor named), Dagne Dover packing for a scenario, Bellroy 'five reasons' listicle, objection cards ('Are crossbody bags safe in crowds?') answered with a demo: all are fresh for Hobo 2.0.
- Footage counts: many moments for zip, pocket, inside, packing, strap, placed, worn, pouch; tie 20 is enough for one chapter; walking, stairs, car are thin and are not used as the picture of a line.
