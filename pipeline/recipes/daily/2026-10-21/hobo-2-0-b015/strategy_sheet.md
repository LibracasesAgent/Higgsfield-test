**Big idea:** A café scene about the one thing nobody solves: where the bag goes when you sit down. The Hobo 2.0's table strap loops round the chair, so the bag stays right where you left it, and the lockable zippers and cut-resistant strap back it up.
**Why it is new:** B013 listed the Hobo 2.0's security features. This turns the single most visual one, the table strap, into the whole story with real footage of the strap looped round a chair and table leg, and puts the other features behind it as a layered answer.

## P02 · Security, layer by layer
**Big idea:** A bag is only as safe as its weakest spot, so the Hobo 2.0 is built in layers: the zippers lock, the strap resists cuts, the table strap anchors it to a chair, the lining blocks card scanners, the pockets stay hidden. One layer per beat, each shown in use.
**Why it is new:** A layered argument (each beat adds one more thing a thief would have to get past) instead of a list or a day: the structure is a build-up, and the picture changes with every layer (zip, strap, chair, lining, pocket).

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [promise] `WEAKEST SPOT`: A bag is only as safe as its weakest spot. Look at this one.  _(picture: zip, strap)_
- H2 [question] `ONE LOCK?`: Why does your bag only have one way to close?  _(picture: lock, zip)_
- H3 [problem] `THREE WEAK SPOTS`: Zips open, straps get cut, cards get scanned. This bag plans for all three.  _(picture: lock, strap, inside)_
- H4 [price] `HALF PRICE`: Black Friday: a bag with layers, at half price.  _(picture: hero, lock)_
