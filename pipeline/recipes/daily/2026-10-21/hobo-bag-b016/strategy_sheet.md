# Strategy sheet: Hobo Bag · black friday · 2026-10-21

## Research and what it changed

- Julian (media buyer, 2026-10-21): wants videos with a model or character sitting and talking, the footage cutting in between, like the mom in the winners; the owner approved avatars when the story needs actors.
- Winner RAWs (94-H4, 123-H4, 147-H1): the proven shape is one person telling a story to camera with b-roll cuts; here the person is a generated host, not a customer, so every line is in 'you' form (Higgsfield's own UGC rules: a generated creator is a host, never a synthetic testimonial).
- B014 (packout, behind, giftcheck, fivechecks) and B015 (chairtie, layers): none had a presenter and none used a person-by-person, step-by-step, myth-and-answer or farewell structure.
- docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: Black Friday ads win on a clear offer and a story; no fake urgency, no sale end date; the offer is fifty percent off + a free matching pouch wallet + free US shipping.
- Test 2026-10-21: Kling 3.0 takes (about 2 credits a second) speak the written line exactly, with natural lips and one steady voice across scenes; the real bag stays faithful when made from its packshot.
