# Strategy sheet: Hobo Bag · black friday · 2026-10-21

## Research and what it changed

- Reviewed what B004-B013 (Hobo Bag) already did: angles n_things, demo, stop_x, travel, value and the day story, and their hooks ('Four things you'll notice', 'Stop digging for your keys', 'Watch what this bag does...', 'Ever pat your bag...', 'Half price, plus a free matching pouch wallet'): none of the four stories below repeats an angle, a hook or a line.
- Read the owner's feedback (Julian): b-roll must match the words, no review chapters, better end cards, videos that look alike: so each project has a different structure (pack-out demo, crowd scenario, gift guide, numbered buyer's guide) and every line names the picture it needs.
- Research (docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md): BFCM statics win with one big visual and one offer line; pack-for-a-scenario demos (Dagne Dover, Bellroy), 'locks to furniture' / feature pointouts (Pacsafe), numbered '5 reasons' (Bellroy), gifting (Fossil), 'don't buy yet' hooks: used for P01, P02, P04, P03.
- Static concepts from the same research: flat-lay 'what fits', café chair with props, pocket close-up, gift styling, dark luxe Black Friday hero, entryway; at least half of the statics are AI studio scenes of the real bag with room left for the words.
- Compliance: only the offer's facts (50% off, free matching pouch wallet, free US shipping, one-year warranty, easy returns, 'anti-theft'), no countdown or sale end date, no 'theft-proof', no first person, no review chapters in the videos.

**Big idea:** A pack-out demo: one day's worth of things go into the Hobo Bag one by one, each into the compartment, zip or pocket made for it, and the bag is carried off three ways.
**Why it is new:** Earlier batches talked features in a list (n_things / demo / stop / travel) or walked a day. This is a visual packing sequence where every line is an item going into a place, ending on the free pouch wallet: a different structure and a different picture for every line.

## P02 · Where your bag is weakest

**Why it is new:** Security has only been a list item in earlier batches ('stop clutching your bag'). This makes it the whole story: a packed-platform scenario, the pocket's position as the hook, and a closing line that it still looks like a handbag.

## P04 · Five checks before you buy any bag
**Big idea:** A buyer's guide with a contrarian hook: five numbered checks to run on any bag before paying, each answered by one real feature of the Hobo Bag, with the verdict 'five for five'.
**Why it is new:** The first numbered, consumer-advice structure: it opens by telling the viewer NOT to buy yet, each chapter is a question plus what to look for plus the Hobo Bag's answer, and the checks run through the product in a fixed order, so it reads as advice and not as a feature list.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [problem] `CHECK FIRST`: Do not buy a bag this Black Friday before you check these five things.  _(picture: hero, handheld)_
- H2 [question] `KEYS TEST`: Can you reach your keys without opening your bag?  _(picture: side_zip, zip_hand)_
- H3 [promise] `5 THINGS`: Five things that tell you if a bag is worth it.  _(picture: inside, pocket, strap)_
- H4 [price] `HALF PRICE`: Black Friday: the bag that passes every check, at half price.  _(picture: hero, pouch)_
