## P01 · One for you, one for her
**Big idea:** Split the three pieces between two women: the hobo is yours, the crossbody is hers, the matching pouch goes in either bag, then go bigger with two bags for the Black Friday bundle price.
**Why it is new:** First 3-Piece Set batch with a share-the-set structure and the client's bundle idea (two bags together, free jewellery box); the earlier set material was a 'which would you choose' video and three statics.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [promise] `YOU AND HER`: One for you, one for her. Black Friday prices are live.  _(picture: hero, colours)_
- H2 [question] `TREAT BOTH`: Shopping for her, and a little for yourself too?  _(picture: hero, placed)_
- H3 [problem] `DO BOTH`: Stop choosing between treating her and treating yourself.  _(picture: hero, handheld)_
- H4 [price] `60% OFF BUNDLE`: Two bags, sixty percent off, and a free jewellery box.  _(picture: hero, colours)_
