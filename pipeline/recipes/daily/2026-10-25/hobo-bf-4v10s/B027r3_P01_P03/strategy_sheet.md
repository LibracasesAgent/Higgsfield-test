# Strategy sheet: Hobo Bag · black friday · 2026-10-25

## Research and what it changed

- Read the strategy packet (true facts, the Black Friday Sale 2026 campaign facts, the footage tags, B004-B020 for the Hobo Bag) and docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: BFCM statics win with one big visual and one offer line; gifting formats; hooks that state the offer in the first line; no countdown graphics.
- The client's four ideas are the four projects, one each: the second bag at the 60% two-bag price (P01), one for you / one for her (P02), Black Friday prices are live now (P03), Christmas gifts for mum, sister and best friend (P04). The gift terms are only the campaign's (Wouter 2026-10-23, data/campaign.json): 2 bags = free mirrored jewellery box, 3+ bags = jewellery box + free cosmetic bag, 60% off only of 2 or 3 bags together, only bags count, while stocks last.
- Earlier Hobo Bag batches did a pack-out, a behind-you demo, a gift checklist (three boxes), five checks, three people on a list, a Black Friday walk, myths and a retirement for the old bag (B014/B016), and the winners remixes (B019/B020): none built the order around the second bag, a you/her pairing, three buyer questions, or the three-bag tier, so those are the four structures here.
- Compliance choices: no sale end date, no countdown, no first person, nothing about what a mum, sister or friend said or felt; recipients appear only as names on a list ('for mum'), the voice is a host in 'you' form; jewellery box and cosmetic bag appear only as the campaign states them; no price numbers (they change).

## P01 · Add a second bag
**Big idea:** Build the order step by step: one Hobo Bag at its own Black Friday price with the free matching pouch wallet, then add a second and the 60% bundle price applies.
**Why it is new:** First Hobo Bag video built as an order ladder around the second bag and the 60% two-bag bundle price; earlier Black Friday videos sold one bag at 50% and never the 2-bag bundle.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [price] `BUY TWO TOGETHER`: Buy two bags together this Black Friday: up to sixty percent off.  _(picture: hero, colours)_
- H2 [question] `ADD A SECOND`: What does a second bag add to your Black Friday order?  _(picture: handheld, colours)_
- H3 [problem] `TWO BAGS`: Stop checking out with one bag when two get a bigger discount.  _(picture: hero, placed)_
- H4 [promise] `SECOND BAG`: The Hobo Bag in your cart gets better with a second one.  _(picture: strap, hero)_

**Why it is new:** First Hobo Bag video built as a question-and-answer for the shopper who is waiting; earlier Black Friday videos were a crowd walk and myths, never a direct 'it is live, no need to wait' answer with the 1-bag and 2-bag deals side by side.

**Why it is new:** First Hobo Bag video that goes name by name down a Christmas list and ends on the three-bag tier (60% off, jewellery box and cosmetic bag); the earlier gift videos used a three-box checklist and three kinds of people, never the campaign's 3-bag gifts.
