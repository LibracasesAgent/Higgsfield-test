## Research and what it changed

- docs/research/2026-11-story-statics.md: the long-running statics carry 5-6 layers; 4 story statics (hook, quote, list, stack: one per review the set has) carry them in our own type
- Wouter 2026-11-04: every static shows the 60% offer; the offer-led formats (set lineup, sticker, stamp, ribbon, window decal, colour split) and the owner's favourites, each once; one format of our own (the cafe chalkboard special)

- Owner 2026-10-26: sell the set (the 'Best Value' upgrade): every static is the whole set, never one bag; 2 concept scenes also ship as motion stills (snow and steam, firelight)
