**Big idea:** A live challenge on a coffee run: can the bag shut, with the coffee still in one hand and the other hand all it gets?
**Why it is new:** B012 listed features in a row; this is one test done on screen, the clasp proves itself in a held shot with its own sound, and the sale only closes it.
