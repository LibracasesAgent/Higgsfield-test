## P01 · Myth: a small bag cannot carry a day (not produced in this batch)
**Big idea:** A myth-versus-fact story about a small bag that holds a whole day; kept in the sheet only as the carrier of the statics, no video is made from it here.
**Why it is new:** No earlier Vintage batch used the myth-versus-fact structure, and none led with the room-for-everything feature over a small-bag myth.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [question] `THE MYTH`: Think a small bag can't hold your day?  _(picture: snap, handheld)_
- H2 [problem] `HANDS FULL`: Both hands busy, and the bag is hanging open.  _(picture: worn, walking)_
- H3 [promise] `TWO WAYS`: One strap, two ways to carry it.  _(picture: strap, handheld)_
- H4 [promise] `ITS OWN SPOT`: Phone, wallet and keys, each in its own spot.  _(picture: packing, inside)_
