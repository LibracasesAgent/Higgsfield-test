## Research and what it changed

- Read docs/research/2026-10-black-friday-and-bag-ads.md: statics win with one big visual and one offer line; flat lays, gift styling and 'what fits' scenes beat busy lifestyle (Bluedrop, Promer, Bellroy, Dagne Dover); kept every static to one idea, minimal words.

- Read the Black Friday Sale 2026 facts: 60% only for 2-3 bags together, free jewellery box with 2 bags, sale runs to 30 November; the Hobo Bag itself stays 50% off. ## P01 · One for you, one for her
**Big idea:** A bundle walk-through: pick two Hobo Bags in two colours, one for you and one for her, and see what each bag does and what two bags unlock this Black Friday.
**Why it is new:** No earlier batch told the bundle story: two bags, 60% off together, the free jewellery box, in the order of the bag's features.

**Hooks (4 versions, same body):**
- H1 [promise] `TWO BAGS DEAL`: Buy two Hobo Bags this Black Friday: up to sixty percent off.  _(picture: colours, hero)_
- H2 [question] `ONE FOR HER`: Who gets the second Hobo Bag on your list?  _(picture: handheld, colours)_
- H3 [price] `BUNDLE DEAL`: Sixty percent off the regular price when you buy two bags together.  _(picture: hero, colours)_
- H4 [problem] `ONE ORDER`: Shopping for her and for you? Do it in one order.  _(picture: pouch, handheld)_
